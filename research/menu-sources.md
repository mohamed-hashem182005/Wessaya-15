# Menu Sources — وصايه الـــ 15

**Checked:** 29 October 2026  
**Primary menu source:** Menu Egypt — [restaurant page](https://menuegypt.com/ar/وصايه-الـــ-15/سوهاج)  
**Source last updated:** 2025-10-09  
**Menu format:** 7 photographed/designed menu pages (watermarked “Menu Egypt”). The images were downloaded for transcription only and are **not** published on the website.

## Source hierarchy

1. **Menu Egypt menu scans (2025-10-09)** — the only accessible source with item names and prices. Used for the structured menu.
2. **Official Facebook recent posts (Sep–Oct 2026)** — confirms the restaurant is active and lists delivery numbers; **no prices** in post text.
3. **Elmenus branch page** — lists the restaurant and cuisines, but online ordering is disabled and no menu item data was exposed in the public page.
4. **RestaurantGuru menu** — uploaded by users about 3 years ago; not used because it is older and not authoritative.

No newer official price list was found. No conflicting current price list was found. Therefore the site presents the 2025-10-09 prices with a visible confirmation note rather than silently treating them as current.

## Transcription rules

- Prices are in **Egyptian pounds (EGP / ج.م)** exactly as printed.
- Where a row has S/M/L or M/L columns, only the printed sizes are included. Missing sizes are omitted, not guessed.
- Items whose prices are not legible or not printed are omitted rather than assigned a price.
- Descriptions are included only when the menu prints them; they are lightly normalised for spelling/spacing but not embellished.
- “NEW” markers are preserved as a `new` flag only where the scan shows them.
- The site does **not** show unavailable/out-of-stock badges because availability is not confirmed.
- Delivery fees, minimum order, and delivery areas are not menu data and are not invented.
