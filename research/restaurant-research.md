# وصايه الـــ 15 — Restaurant Research

**Research date:** 29 October 2026  
**Primary lead:** https://www.facebook.com/Wessaya15.st/  
**Project language:** Arabic-first (RTL), with the verified English brand name “Wessaya 15”.

## Access note — be precise about what was checked

The Facebook page was **not browsed through a logged-in session**. The public page HTML was fetched with a crawler user agent. That returned public page metadata, the public intro card, recent feed post text, the cover/profile media references, and an official WhatsApp link. It did **not** expose the full photo albums, all pinned posts, comments, or the complete historical timeline. Six recent reel thumbnails were retrieved; those are the only Facebook post images used in this project.

I did **not** claim to have watched the reels/videos. The still thumbnails and post captions were used as visual research only.

## Recent official post activity (public feed)

The public HTML contained 20 recent posts dated **10 September 2026 – 28 October 2026**. Captions reference pizza quality, grilled chicken, burgers, fried-chicken sandwiches, family meals, a football prediction giveaway, and hiring. All ordering captions repeat the same four delivery numbers. No post text contained menu prices.

Six recent reel thumbnails were retrieved and used as authentic food/interior photography. They show pizza preparation, a grilled chicken meal, a burger, pizza close-ups, and the dining room. They are **not** treated as photographs of specific named menu items unless the caption makes that clear.

## Conflicts and unreliable data

- **Name spelling:** official page “وصايه”; common usage “وصاية”. Resolved by using the official spelling visually and both spellings in SEO alternate names.
- **Price range:** RestaurantGuru claims “EGP 560–1,400 per person”. This is implausible for the published menu (most items EGP 10–780, with family meal EGP 780) and is not official. Not used.
- **Opening hours:** two directories agree on 10:00–02:00, but the official page did not expose hours in the public HTML. Treated as unconfirmed.
- **Menu freshness:** Menu Egypt scans were last updated **2025-10-09**. No newer price list was found on the public Facebook feed or other accessible sources. Prices are presented as the latest available published menu, with a visible “confirm before ordering” note.
- **Delivery coverage:** Menu Egypt lists Sohag and Tama, but this is a directory entry, not an official delivery policy. The site uses a free-text area field and asks WhatsApp to confirm coverage/fees.
